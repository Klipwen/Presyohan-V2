🚨 1. The 3 Technical Code Blockers
Missing Camera Permission (AndroidManifest.xml)

Problem: Camera/scanning features request Manifest.permission.CAMERA in code, but the permission is not declared in AndroidManifest.xml.
Result: Camera will crash or silently fail on real Android devices.
Missing In-App Account Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

Problem: Google Play policy strictly mandates that any app allowing user signup (SignupActivity) MUST provide:
An in-app button to permanently delete their account and data.
A public webpage link for users to request account deletion online.
Result: Automatic Play Console app review rejection.
Release Build & App Protection (build.gradle.kts)

Problem: Minification/Obfuscation is turned off (isMinifyEnabled = false), and release signing (.jks keystore) is not yet configured.
Result: Larger bundle size, unprotected code, and inability to build signed production bundles (.aab).
